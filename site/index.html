Quick answers

Which phones does it work on?+

iPhone and Android. This website just tells you about it.

Do I have to set everything up by hand?+

No. Describe your week in your own words, as text or a voice note, or import a CSV, and Dayli builds the first version for you. You can still add things one by one.

What's the difference between fixed and flexible?+

Fixed things have a set time, like work or a class. Flexible things have a "how often", like the gym four times a week, and Dayli fits them into your free time.
